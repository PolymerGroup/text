Analytical & Compliance FAQ

What analytical methods verify your peptide purity and identity?

Every production lot is tested by independent third-party laboratories using Reversed-Phase High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (RP-HPLC) at 214 nm / 220 nm to quantify target peak purity (≥99.0%), alongside Liquid Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (LC-MS) to confirm exact molecular weight.

How should lyophilized peptides be stored upon receipt?

Lyophilized powders remain stable at ambient temperatures during expedited transit. Upon receipt, store sealed vials desiccated at −20°C or colder (−80°C for long-term archival beyond 6 months) and protect from UV light.

What are these reference materials intended for?

All materials are supplied strictly for legitimate in-vitro laboratory research, assay development, and analytical reference validation by qualified professionals. Not for human or veterinary consumption, clinical, or therapeutic application.
